\begin{abstract}
We register a formally specified and machine-checked kernel for a federated
automated adjudication system in which independent computational lines judge
mathematical and operational claims. The kernel comprises: (i) a three-rung,
three-track credibility lattice obtained as the free completion of a legacy
five-element poset, verified by exhaustive enumeration over all $11^3=1331$
ordered triples; (ii) a five-state certificate lifecycle machine with nine
legal transitions and three machine-checked invariants; (iii) a certified
numerical anchor: uniqueness of the gauged fixed point of a circulant
entropic-optimal-transport (Sinkhorn) system, established by a Krawczyk
interval operator with outward rounding, and ported to the Lean~4 verified
interval library \emph{leancert}; and (iv) a formal bridge from domain-limit
necessity to Rice's theorem, machine-verified in Lean~4/Mathlib on cloud
infrastructure. Empirical fuzzing observed zero false accepts; we report
rule-of-three upper bounds and do not claim a guarantee.
\end{abstract}

\section{A certified numerical anchor}
For the circulant cost $C[i][j]=c_{(j-i)\bmod 6}$ with
$c=(\tfrac12,\tfrac13,\tfrac23,\tfrac14,\tfrac34,\tfrac15)$, regularization
$\varepsilon=1$, uniform marginals, and gauge $f_0=0$, the fixed-point system
\[
f_i+\log 6+\log\textstyle\sum_j e^{g_j-C[i][j]}=0,\qquad
g_j+\log 6+\log\textstyle\sum_i e^{f_i-C[i][j]}=0
\]
has the closed-form solution $f^*=0$, $g^*=-(\log 6+\mathrm{lse}(-c))$.
